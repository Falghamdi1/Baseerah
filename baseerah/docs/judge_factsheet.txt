THE PROBLEM
  Saudi households lose thousands of SAR/year to invisible drains.
  They feel it on bills — months later — when it's too late to act.

THE SOLUTION
  Answer plain questions. See 3 futures. Pick one.
  No kWh. No litres. No degrees. Just chips and sliders.

WHAT IT SIMULATES
  ● Food waste    ● Electricity    ● Water

3 STRATEGIES SIMULTANEOUSLY
  📍 Current    ✨ Optimized    🔥 Aggressive

4 TIME HORIZONS
  3 Months  ·  6 Months  ·  1 Year  ·  3 Years

  Electricity   Linear regression  (AC hours + appliances + laundry)

  Water         Component model    (shower + laundry + irrigation)
